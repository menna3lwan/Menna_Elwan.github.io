About Me

Turning ideas into production‑ready mobile apps

I'm a Flutter Developer with hands-on experience building cross-platform mobile applications using Flutter and Dart, backed by a strong command of Clean Architecture and state management (BLoC, Provider, Riverpod). I care about code that stays clean and scalable as products grow, not just code that works on day one.

Beyond internships and freelance-style builds, I contribute to the Flutter open-source ecosystem — customizing and integrating PlutoGrid, a widely used data-grid package, into production screens. I'm currently completing my B.Sc. in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence at Benha University.
